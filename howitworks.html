Folding Styles

Recommended

If unsure or do not have a preference, let us use our experience to determine the best fold for your garments.

KonMari

Our default folding technique, developed by Mari Kondo, KonMari is a fold that turns garments into small, sturdy rectangles that stand upright in drawers often using dividers.

Military Roll

The most space efficient fold, the Military Roll is a folding technique used to make clothing extremely compact, wrinkle-free, and self-securing. Perfect is packing clothing before a flight!

Hot Topic

A classic drawer style rectangle fold where the front of garments are visible and stacked upright.

Shelf Style

The traditional way of folding towels & wash clothes for storage is a closet. Often suitable for wire rack.

Combine Styles

The clothing bags we provide come with a tag. This tag has labels: room, name, notes, and fold type!
