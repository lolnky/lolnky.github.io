Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve the Northern Kentucky (NKY) area, bringing convenience right to your doorstep.

How does pickup and delivery work?

Simply schedule a pickup online, and we'll collect your laundry from your porch or rooms. We'll wash, dry, and fold it, then deliver it back fresh and clean - as instructed.

Can I request specific detergents or washing preferences?

Absolutely! We cater to your needs, offering fragrance-free and hypoallergenic options. Just let us know your preferences.

How is pricing determined?

Our pricing is weight-based, ensuring transparency. You'll receive a clear estimate before we begin.

What if I need to reschedule?

Easy! Contact us via email or text 3 hours before service is scheduled.

We will reschedule pickups and deliveries - to an extent. If attempts continue to fail, pickups may be cancelled. For deliveries, your property will be ultimately returned to the address provided.
